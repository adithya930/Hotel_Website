About Us

Grand Palace Hotel

The five-star Grand Mountain hotel is especially designed to offer the best in comfort and convenience with a dedicated team whose wealth of experience and expertise will ensure exceptional standards of service and care to all our guests. With plush amenities for all indoor and outdoor activities, the hotel serves local and international cuisines. The pool in the central courtyard is large enough to entertain both adults and children. Along with a well equipped spacious Gymnasium and ultra modern Spa, a must-see illuminated water fountain display accompanied by music sums up a few of the highlights and can be enjoyed by all guests
